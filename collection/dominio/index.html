Dominio

The Mafia Boss

Doubt ends where control begins.

Formula — first working iteration (v1)

A composition built around control: a smoke-cut citrus opening collapses into a dark leather-rose heart, and settles into a long, dry oud-and-amber base. Built for winter evenings and rooms where restraint reads louder than volume.

Character

Opening

Dominio opens cold: a curl of smoke over bitter grapefruit and bergamot rind, cut immediately by black and pink pepper. There is no soft entrance. The citrus never reads as fresh — it reads as a threat wrapped in something bright, gone within minutes, like a match struck and put out.

Heart

The center is a dark rose, not a pretty one — Bulgarian rose pressed into leather, tobacco leaf and cold frankincense, with geranium adding a green, bitter edge and patchouli pulling it down into shadow. This is the room after the meeting: velvet and smoke, still guarded.

Drydown

Oud and vetiver hold the composition dry and earthbound long after the top has burned off, while vanilla and benzoin move in slowly beneath it — not sweet, warm. What remains on skin hours later is less a scent than a residue of presence: something was here, and it has not fully left.
